What is Ulcerative colitis?

Ulcerative Colitis is a chronic Inflammatory Bowel Disease in which abnormal reactions of the immune system causes inflammation, irritation and ulcers on the inner lining of your large intestine. It affects innermost lining of large intestine, that is colon and rectum. In most people, symptoms usually develop over a period of time, rather than suddenly. It is an autoimmune disorder, which means the body’s immune system attacks healthy tissues. But some research suggests that the chronic inflammation may not be due to the immune system attacking the body itself, instead, it is a result of the immune system attacking a harmless virus, bacteria, or food in the gut, causing inflammation that leads to bowel injury
Ulcerative Colitis is a condition in which there is inflammation and ulcers in the large intestine.  It affects the innermost lining of the colon (large intestine). Ulcerative Colitis may cause cramping, abdominal pain, bloating gas, diarrhoea mixed with blood, Weight loss, fever and anaemia.

Symptoms of Ulcerative Colitis

• Diarrhoea, often with blood, mucus or pus.
• Rectal bleeding — passing small amount of blood along with stool.
• Abdominal pain and cramping.
• Rectal pain.
• Tiredness.
• Nausea
• Urgency to defecate.
• Inability to defecate despite urgency.
• Weight loss.
• Fatigue.
• Anaemia
• Fever
• Mouth sores

Complications of Ulcerative colitis

Arthritis: Osteoporosis, low bone mass, pain, swelling and stiffness of the joints – which usually happens in larger joints, such as the ankles, knees, hips and wrist.
Inflammation of the Skin, Eyes and Joints tender bumps can appear on the skin – usually on the arms and legs. eyes can become red, irritated and itchy.
Painful mouth ulcers.
Liver Disease
Anemia, a condition in which you have fewer red blood cells than normal.
Colorectal Cancer
Toxic megacolon. Ulcerative colitis may cause the colon to rapidly widen and swell, a serious condition known as toxic megacolon.
A hole in the colon (perforated colon). A perforated colon most commonly is caused by toxic megacolon, but it may also occur on its own.
Risk of colon cancer.
